Halloway Street, 1893. The house was cheap. The cellar was not on the deeds. Behind its oak door, twelve brick steps become thirteen, and the thirteenth keeps going.

You are the overseer. You have a lamp that lets you see them, a purse that is never enough, and a household of people with nothing left to lose. Send four down. Bring back what you can.
