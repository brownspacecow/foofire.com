ROOF RIDER

Three streets of rooftop rhythm sections. The fast line is never the flat ground: it is the backside of the next roof.

1 · LOAD Hold Space before the gable. The suspension squats and stores energy.
2 · POP Let go just before the ridge for a bigger, longer jump.
3 · SCRUB Hold Space in the air with the bike level to flatten the arc.
4 · LAND Touch down on the green backside line and keep your flow.

How to ride a roofline

  • Preload early. Holding Space compresses the suspension. Release it just before the gable and the bike pops.
  • Scrub when you are hot. Holding Space in the air flattens the arc. That is how you stop over-jumping the next roof.
  • Find the green. The dashed line on each landing roof is the backside. A backside landing adds flow points; flat or cased landings break the chain.
  • Keep the wheels under you. Lean with A and D in the air, and press R to go back to the last checkpoint.
  • Race your ghost. Finish a track to save your fastest run. On the next run your best line rides with you as a faded bike marked BEST.
