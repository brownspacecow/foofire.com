Pizza Homestead

Fun Room Rush

Mom gave you one hour and a rewards program. Every slice you finish earns arcade money. The Fun Room is legendary. Make it count.

  • 🍕 Grab slices from the buffet. Six fit on your plate.
  • 🪑 Sit at a booth and eat: every slice earns $2.
  • 🕹️ Spend it in the Fun Room: $2 a play, then WHACK Hank.
  • 🧾 Avoid Manager Brenda. She has a clipboard and nothing to lose.

Move: WASD / arrows or drag. Eat or play: tap or press Space. Whack again to hit Hank.
