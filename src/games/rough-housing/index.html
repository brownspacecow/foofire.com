Rough Housing

One player builds the arena out of ten Nuggets. Then both players climb on and try to bump, jump and cartwheel the other into the lava.

  • 🛋️Pose bases, hinged cushions and triangle pillows into forts.
  • 🤼No punches, no shoves: momentum does the hitting.
  • 🌋Last wrestler on the foam scores a point. First to 3 wins.

Build with the mouse and the pose keys. Fight with WASD + Space + Shift and ← ↑ → ↓ + RShift + /.
