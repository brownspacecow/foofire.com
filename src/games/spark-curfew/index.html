Spark Curfew

One night. One yard. One very nosy street.

The neighbours said keep it down. You heard "put on a show." Buy fireworks, build a crowd, and save up for Big Bertha. The sooner she goes up, the bigger the time bonus.

  • 🛒 Buy from the stand. Whatever you own goes in the crate.
  • 🔥 Tap the yard to set a firework down, then tap it again to light the fuse.
  • ☀️ Little fireworks bring the daylight with them. The big shells only go up at night.
  • 🧨 Fireworks placed close together share a fuse. Light one and its neighbours catch, so you can chain a whole cluster in one tap.
  • ⚠️ Keep the chain going for bigger payouts — but five or more in one chain can misfire and cost you cash, crowd and the chain.
  • 💥 Big Bertha is the super grand finale, but she only comes out once the whole street is watching. Pack the yard to unlock her, then light her fast for a time bonus.

Tap or click to place and light. Keys 1–8 pick a firework you own, Space lights the newest one, Esc cancels.

No downloads. No accounts. Definitely do not try this in a real yard.
