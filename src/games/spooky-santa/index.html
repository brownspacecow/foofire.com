Spooky Santa

A very quiet delivery

You are three feet from the tree and every floorboard in this house has opinions. Deliver all five presents, then get back up the chimney before sunrise.

  • 🎁 Stand on a glowing spot and press action to leave a present.
  • 🤫 Sneak to stay quiet. Running fills the jingle meter and wakes the house.
  • 🔦 Stay out of Dad's flashlight, the kid's stare and the tree's sweeping lights.
  • 🛋️ Duck behind the couch, into the hall closet or the pantry to vanish.
  • 🚪 Step onto a glowing green closet floor: nobody can see you while you stand there.
  • 🍪 Eat the kitchen cookies for a sugar-rush dash. Squeaky toys are loud.

Move: WASD / arrows or drag. Sneak: Shift. Action: Space / E. Mute: M.
