How to play

  • Touch the pad. Left and right play the scale; up and down opens the tone.
  • Pick a sound and a scale — every note is a good one.
  • REC a phrase, then press OVERDUB to stack more layers. Change sounds and the loop changes with them.
  • Keyboard: focus the pad, use the arrows, hold Space to play.
