How to play

Drag through 3+ matching tiles — swords hurt the monster, shields block its bite, potions heal you, coins are treasure! Longer paths are stronger, and chains of 5+ tiles hit even harder! Drag back along your path to undo it and pick a different route. Mom sneaks diagonally, and Dad can hop over one tile that doesn't match, once per chain! Beat the monster to descend. Between floors, spend your gold at the Crypt Shop! A boss waits every 5th floor. If there are no moves left, the crypt shuffles the tiles for free. Rumour says something REALLY big waits at the bottom of the crypt...
