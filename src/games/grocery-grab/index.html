How to play

Steer your cart with WASD or the arrow keys — or drag anywhere on the store and a joystick pops up under your finger. Grab groceries by rolling over them: everything is worth its price, and items on your shopping list pay an extra $2.00 each. Find all 6 list items for a $5.00 completion bonus, and hunt the 3 golden bonus items — they are worth $15.00 to $25.00 each! Wet floors slow you down, bumping into shoppers wobbles your cart, and a full cart rolls a little slower. Tap Check Out any time for a time bonus, or keep grabbing until the clock hits zero. P pauses, M mutes.
