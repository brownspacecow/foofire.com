Ball Pit Bonanza

Wade into the biggest ball pit in the arcade. Shove the balls around, dig with both hands, and pull out whatever has been lost down there — old watches, soggy pizza, and if you are truly lucky, something brand new that definitely should not be in a ball pit. Sell your haul for coins, then spend them on lucky charms, speedy sneakers and a wilder playground. Fill a display case with your favourite finds and show them off.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or click (or drag) in the pit to walk there. Click a nearby ball, or press Space or F, to dig and fling the balls away — most digs are just balls, but every so often something good pops out. Digging the same spot over and over wears it out, so wade somewhere new: fresh spots are luckier and every five in a row pays an explorer bonus. Keep an eye out for random pit events too — ballalanches, duck parades, golden hours and worse.
