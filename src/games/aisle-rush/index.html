    THE RULEBOOK

    HOW TO PLAY

    1. Pick a team. Your partner waits at the register. The other two teams are your rivals.
    2. Answer six grocery questions. Every correct answer adds 5 seconds to that team's Big Sweep clock. Everyone starts with 0:30.
    3. Drive the Big Sweep. Use WASD or arrow keys, or drag anywhere on the canvas. Roll past shelves to scoop items into your cart.
    4. Know the good stuff. Giant display items are worth $180–$300. Hidden cash is worth up to $500. Poultry, coffee and cleaning products are pricey too.
    5. Play the station challenges. Park at the coffee grinder or the deli slicer and stop the meter in the green for a cash bonus. The Big Sweep clock keeps running while you play.
    6. Bank a full cart. You can carry 24 items. Drive into your team-colour register zone to bank the cart and start fresh. Anything left in the cart still counts when time runs out.
    7. Follow the five-item rule. Five of any one product, then that shelf is done for you.
    8. Win the Big Sweep. The highest checkout total goes to the Bonus Sweep: three clues, 60 seconds, and a $5,000 case of cash waiting at your register.
